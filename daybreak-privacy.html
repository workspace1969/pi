Who We Are

Daybreak is a marketing platform and service from Team Nirvana. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. “We,” “us,” and “our” mean Team Nirvana.

Information You Give Us

When you send a note, request a demo, or use the Search Intent Explorer report, we collect what you enter: your name, email, company, website, the keywords you want to test, and your message.

Information Collected Automatically

Like most websites, ours may record basic technical information such as browser type, device, pages visited, and the site that referred you, through cookies or similar technologies. We use it in aggregate to improve the site.

If We Contacted You

Daybreak helps businesses reach people who have recently searched for their products or services. That information comes from licensed third-party data providers. If you received a message through Daybreak and don’t want to hear from that business again, use the unsubscribe or opt-out option in the message, or contact us below and we’ll add you to our suppression list.

How We Use It

We use your information to reply to you, schedule and run demos, deliver the services our clients hire us for, understand how the site is used, and keep it secure. We do not sell the personal information you give us on this website.

Who We Share It With

We share information only with service providers who help us run the site and our business, such as hosting, scheduling, email, CRM, and analytics providers, and only so they can provide those services. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of our business.

Cookies

We use a small number of cookies to make the site work and to measure how it’s used. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site will still work, though some features may not remember your preferences.

How Long We Keep It

We keep contact and demo information for as long as we have a reason to stay in touch, and delete it when we no longer need it or when you ask us to. Suppression records are kept so we can honor your opt-out.

Your Choices and Rights

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or ask us to stop contacting you. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act. We won’t treat you differently for using them.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No website can guarantee absolute security, so please don’t send anything sensitive through our forms.

Children

This site and our services are meant for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we’ll change the date at the top of this page.

Contact Us

Questions, requests, or opt-outs can be sent through our contact page.

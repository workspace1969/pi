WHAT IS DAYBREAK?

The 3-Step System That Finds Your Buyers, Creates Your Content, and Delivers Your Message by Morning.

Daybreak is an AI-powered marketing platform for small businesses and established brands. B2B and B2C companies use our growth engine to reach prospects already looking for what you sell. Every morning, we pull a list of people who searched for your products or services within the last 24 hours and turn that search intent into conversations through our outreach. You wake up to replies, not to-do lists.

HOW DAYBREAK WORKS

Our AI-Powered Platform Does the Heavy Lifting. Our C-Level Team Makes the Decisions.

After three decades as agency executives and creative leaders, we believe judgment is everything, and AI alone doesn’t have it. We’re with you at every step to make sure your message is the right one, in the right context, shaped by people with the experience to know the difference.

Step 1: Find Buyers

Configure Search Intent

We match your keywords to the searches your buyers actually use when they’re ready to buy.

Pull and Enrich

At 7 a.m., we pull everyone who searched in the last 24 hours, enriched with the best contact info.

Step 2: Create Messaging

Message Strategy

Our C-level team sets the strategy, the voice, and exactly what each audience segment needs to hear.

Write the Content

We write campaigns for each segment, based on the strategy we agreed on and top-performing search terms.

Step 3: Deliver It

Begin Outreach

Email, text, direct mail, voicemail, and paid media, delivered so your audience actually sees it.

Track and Monitor

The dashboard tracks all activity. Top replies go to your sales team. What works feeds the next morning.

Who we are

Team Nirvana is a marketing, creative, and advisory firm. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and what you can do about it. When we say “we,” “us,” or “our,” we mean Team Nirvana.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you fill out our contact form or book a call, we collect what you enter: your name, email address, company, the reason you’re reaching out, and your message.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, ours may record basic technical information such as your browser type, device, pages visited, and the site that referred you. This is collected through cookies or similar technologies and is used in aggregate.

Information from scheduling and other tools. If you book a call, our scheduling provider collects the details you enter there and shares them with us so we can hold the meeting.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to you, to hold the meetings you book, to understand how the site is used so we can improve it, and to keep the site secure. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising on behalf of anyone else.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers who help us run the site and our business, such as hosting, scheduling, email, and analytics providers. They may use it only to provide those services to us. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of our business.

Cookies

We use a small number of cookies to make the site work and to measure how it’s used. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site will still work, though some features may not remember your preferences.

How long we keep it

We keep contact and meeting information for as long as we have a reason to stay in touch, and delete it when we no longer need it or when you ask us to.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or ask us to stop contacting you. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act or the General Data Protection Regulation. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No website can guarantee absolute security, so please don’t send us anything sensitive through the contact form.

Children

This site is meant for business audiences. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Links to other sites

Our site may link to other websites, including our scheduling provider. Their privacy practices are their own, and we encourage you to read their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we’ll change the date at the top of this page.

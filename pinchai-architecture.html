The Architectural Outcome

The Moat Is Not a Warehouse of Identity. It Is a Governed Relationship Layer.

Less raw data in AI workflows means a smaller breach blast radius, with centralized control and full auditability. Pinch converts permissioned context into better decisions and measurable value, without ever warehousing exposed identity.

Step One

Separate

Identity is split from usable context at the source. Raw identifiers never enter AI workflows, which shrinks the breach blast radius from day one.

Step Two

Minimize

AI retrieval runs on scoped tokens and approved attributes only. Less raw data in the workflow means less risk everywhere in the stack.

Step Three

Govern

One control plane enforces consent, access control, audit logs, revocation, deletion, and retention across every signal.

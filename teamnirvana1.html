AI is not the problem.

Everyone knows ChatGPT. Almost no one knows how to use it correctly. Most people give an AI one sentence of direction, get a generic draft back, and blame the tools because it isn’t quite right. A digital camera never made anybody a photographer. It made the good ones faster and better once they learned how to use it. We brief our machines the way we brief a human team: tone, audience, what it must never say, and the context only someone on the account would know. Then we mark it up, send it back to the team, and iterate over and over until it’s right.

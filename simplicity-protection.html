How a Claim Works

No maze. Three simple steps for every covered system and appliance in your home.

1

Call Before the Work Starts

Call (800) 929-5150 and tell us what failed, whether it is the AC, the water heater, or the dishwasher. We open a claim and dispatch a licensed contractor, or you use your own.

2

We Authorize the Covered Repair

The technician diagnoses the problem and calls us. We check it against your contract and authorize the covered repair before you owe anything beyond your service call.

3

We Pay the Contractor Directly

We pay the contractor for the covered work. You pay your service call, the technician finishes the job, cleans up, and leaves, and your home is back to normal the same day.

Common Questions

Is this the same as homeowners insurance?+
No. Homeowners insurance covers damage from events like fire, storms, or theft. Simplicity Home Protection® is a service contract that covers the repair or replacement of your home’s systems and appliances when they fail from everyday use. The two work together.
Can I use my own contractor?+
Yes. Any licensed, bonded, and insured contractor you trust, or we can dispatch one from our vetted network. Just call us before the work starts so we can authorize the covered repair.
Does the age of my home or appliances matter?+
No. There is no age limit on your home, systems, or appliances. Coverage applies as long as the item was working when your contract started and fails from normal wear and use.
Do I need a home inspection to sign up?+
No inspection or maintenance records are required to buy a plan. Pick your property type, choose your options, and select a plan.
What do I pay when something breaks?+
Just the service call fee you chose when you set up your plan: $75, $100, or $125 per visit. We pay the contractor directly for the covered repair or replacement.
What if I change my mind?+
Cancel within 30 days for a full refund if you haven’t filed a claim. After that, you can cancel any time for a prorated refund.
Does the coverage transfer if I sell my home?+
Yes. Your contract can be transferred to the new homeowner, which is a nice selling point at closing. Call us and we’ll handle the paperwork.

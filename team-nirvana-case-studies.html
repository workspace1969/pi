01

RV Storage Brand, Scaled Across the Country

National Indoor Storage

Overview

National Indoor Storage started with one facility in Louisville and an ambition much bigger than one building. RV and boat owners had few good options: outdoor lots, chain-link fences, and no brand anyone trusted with the most valuable thing they owned after their home.

The founders needed more than a logo for a building. They needed a brand that could open in a new market and feel established on day one, without being rebuilt every time.

Solution

We built the brand from the ground up: positioning, name architecture, identity, facility signage, the website, and a launch playbook every new location could follow.

Then we went past storage. We designed and launched their online RV marketplace, giving owners a place to consign and sell through a brand they already trusted with their vehicle. One brand, two lines of business, one repeatable system.

Results

  • One Louisville facility grew into a national operation.
  • The RV marketplace turned storage customers into sellers and buyers.
  • Today they run facilities across the country.

02

Wealth Management Firm, Acquired at a Premium

Quintessence Wealth Management

Overview

Quintessence served high-net-worth families with a level of care the big firms could not match. The brand did not show it. The identity looked like every other advisory firm in the market, and the digital presence was an afterthought.

Leadership knew the firm was worth more than it looked. If a buyer ever came calling, the first impression would be the website, and the website was underselling the business.

Solution

We rebranded the firm around what made it different: the relationships, the discipline, and the quiet confidence its clients already felt. New positioning, a new identity, and a voice that sounded like the partners in the room.

We built the website and launched their digital footprint so that a prospect, a recruit, or an acquirer would all reach the same conclusion within a minute: this firm is a cut above.

Results

  • A brand that finally matched the caliber of the practice.
  • A digital presence that did the first meeting’s work before the first meeting.
  • The presence we built helped position them for acquisition by Tolleson Wealth Management at a premium.

03

Healthcare Staffing Software, Acquired by a Top Competitor

Clearview Staffing Software

Overview

Clearview had a transformational product for healthcare staffing and a hard market to enter. The category was crowded, the incumbents were loud, and the product had to win over three very different audiences at the same time.

One generic website was never going to do it. Each audience had its own problem, its own language, and its own reason to say yes.

Solution

We launched Clearview with three distinct sites for three audience segments, each with its own message, proof, and path to a conversation, all held together by one brand.

Around the sites we built a powerful digital presence: launch campaigns, content, and sales materials that made a new product look like the company the category should be watching. It put their product category on notice.

Results

  • Three audiences, three sites, one brand, launched together.
  • A new entrant that showed up looking like a leader.
  • Their largest competitor acquired them 4 months later.
